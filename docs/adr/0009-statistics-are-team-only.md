# 0009 — Statistics are team-only, never per player

Date: 2026-11-08 · Status: accepted · Context: issue #121 (S12 — Statistics)

Tournament statistics are aggregated per team only: Tichu success counts, bomb counts,
double wins, highlights and team trends. No figure is shown per player. Teammates play
as one team, and a per-player metric would give them a reason to play against each other
to win it, which the tournament does not want to support.

## Why

- Standings are already a team competition. A per-player metric would add a personal
  incentive inside the team that the tournament does not want.
- Team-level figures keep every metric comparable: each team plays four games per round,
  so per-game averages need no sit-out adjustment.
- Hard to reverse once teams have seen per-player numbers, and a future reader would
  reasonably ask why no player statistics exist.
