# Tichu Rules

The rules of a single deal of Tichu, shared by every app that records Tichu: what a
game consists of and how it is scored. Apps decide what groups games together.

## Language

**Game**:
One completed deal of Tichu between two teams of two players, recorded as the four
players' positions, Tichu calls, bombs played and the two teams' base scores.
_Avoid_: hand, match, round (when speaking of a single deal)

**Position**:
A player's finish order within one game (1 = first out). In a double win the two
winning players hold positions 1 and 2; the two losing players have no position —
the game ended without them finishing.
_Avoid_: rank, placement

**Tichu Call**:
A player's pre-deal announcement: none, small Tichu (after first card) or grand
Tichu (before any card). Success is derived: called and finished first. A small
Tichu wins or loses 100 points; a grand Tichu the **Grand Tichu Bonus**, 200 unless
an app scores it like a small Tichu.
_Avoid_: announcement, bet

**Base Score**:
The card points a team collects in a game before bonuses; the two teams' base scores
sum to 100. The only score entered — bonuses and totals are derived.
_Avoid_: raw score, card points

**Double Win**:
A game outcome where one team's two players finish first and second; the base scores
are 0/0 and the other team's players have no position. The winning team earns the
**Double Win Bonus**, 200 unless an app halves it.
_Avoid_: sweep, shutout

**Game Score**:
A team's base score plus its Tichu and Double Win bonuses in one game.
_Avoid_: total, points

**Bombs Played**:
The number of bombs one player played in a game.
_Avoid_: bomb count (a turnier statistic)

**Beschiss**:
A per-game flag meaning "someone cheated — sort of": the loose, playful Tichu-table
accusation, not a formal ruling. Set when entering the game; carries no scoring
consequence.
_Avoid_: cheating (too grave), foul
